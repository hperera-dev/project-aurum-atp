THE THREE-PART PROBLEM

Why paper receipts and siloed retail software fail

Sri Lankan buyers purchase gold across multiple jewellers, yet post-purchase records remain trapped on fading paper or inside isolated single-shop systems.

1. Fragmented Customer Records

Paper bills, warranties, and assay slips fade or get lost over decades. Customers who buy from multiple shops have no single private vault holding purchase proofs, repair histories, or redesign lineage.

2. Competitor Surveillance Risk

Jewellers need reliable records for items they sold (servicing, resizing, code recovery). However, no shop will join a shared platform if competitor Shop B can view Shop A’s sales or customer totals.

3. Scattered Gold Information

Buyers struggle to distinguish official Central Bank reference gold rates and NGJA hallmarking rules from promotional shop pricing or misleading financial claims.

SYSTEM LIFECYCLE

Verified shop-to-customer handover without exposing data

AURUM replaces unauthenticated public QR lookups with an atomic two-sided handshake and tenant-isolated PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS).

Opaque Physical Handle Binding

Authenticated jeweller staff enter declared specifications (category, gross/net weight, fineness like 22K/916, reference photos) in the Retailer Web Portal.

  • Generates a permanent internal asset_uuid scoped to the issuing shop.
  • Binds a random, revocable PhysicalCode handle (never NIC, name, or price).
  • Specs are transparently labelled as Declared unless official assay certificates are attached.

Two-Sided Consent-Based Checkout

Ownership never changes silently behind a customer's back. At checkout, both the issuing shop and the buyer participate in an atomic handshake.

  • Shop A staff scans the mark, verifies unsold stock status, and sends a short-lived in-app invite.
  • Customer opens the AURUM Flutter app, previews item specs, and explicitly taps Accept or Decline.
  • One idempotent database transaction updates the Customer Vault, logs Shop A’s sale ledger, and writes an immutable audit event.
