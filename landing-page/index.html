THE SRI LANKAN GOLD INDUSTRY GAP

The 3-Part Problem & How AURUM Solves It

Gold is Sri Lanka's ultimate generational asset, yet the relationship between jewellery shops, paper bills, and customer privacy remains broken. Here is how Project AURUM bridges the gap:

1

Fragmented Paper Receipts

Families buy jewellery over 30+ years across multiple reputable shops (Raja, Devi, Swarna, Lalitha). Receipts, paper warranty slips, and assay cards fade, tear, or get lost in drawers. When visiting a shop for repair, resizing, or pawning, owners have no unified provenance record.

AURUM Solution

Private Cross-Shop Vault: A single, unified lifetime digital wallet where all purchased items from any participating jeweller appear side-by-side with untouched original invoice PDFs locked in AES-256 encrypted storage.

2

Retailer Surveillance Fear

Jewellers refuse shared platforms because they fear competitor espionage: if Shop B can see Shop A’s inventory, pricing margins, or high-net-worth client lists, the business is compromised. Traditional centralized databases create severe commercial trust barriers.

AURUM Solution

Isolated Shop Ledgers & Atomic Handover: Enforced by strict PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS). Shop B can never see Shop A's sales or customer portfolio. Handover requires a dual-authenticated POS handshake with explicit buyer consent.

3

Scattered Gold Information

Sri Lankan gold buyers struggle to find authentic Central Bank reference rates versus Sea Street retail markups. Misleading marketing, fluctuating pawning loan-to-value ratios, and confusing hallmarking terms leave consumers vulnerable to deceptive practices.

AURUM Solution

Curated Gold News & CBSL Hub: An ad-free, verified daily feed pulling official Central Bank of Sri Lanka (CBSL) 22K/24K prices, National Gem & Jewellery Authority (NGJA) assay standards, and educational guides directly into the app.

03 • Retailer Partner Web Portal (Showroom POS)

Blind Tenant-Isolated Scan & Sell POS

Built for showroom cashiers and branch managers with shared-screen privacy enforced at every step. Staff scan physical DataMatrix marks or customer Counter Pass QRs, group multi-item invoices with combined net gold mass, attach untouched VAT invoice PDFs, and dispatch dual-consented handovers.

STATUTORY DATA PROTECTION NOTICE

Mandatory Privacy & Data Ethics Notice

In accordance with the Sri Lanka Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) No. 9 of 2022 and University of Westminster research ethics guidelines:

1. Nature and Purpose of Data Collected

When you register on this landing page, we collect your Full Name, Email Address, and optional Retail Showroom Name. This data is collected solely to notify you when the AURUM mobile application enters closed beta testing and to allow examiners to verify the working status of our Call-to-Action during project assessment.

2. Strict Zero-Home-Address Architectural Guarantee

To eliminate the risk of physical robbery honeypots (e.g. database leaks associating high-value gold collections with residential street addresses), Project AURUM has zero database columns for home addresses, postal codes, or GPS coordinates. Retailers maintain billing addresses inside their independent legacy ERPs; AURUM never ingests them.

3. Data Retention and Encryption Standard

All submitted waitlist records are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and stored using AES-256 encryption. We never sell, rent, or share personal data with third-party advertisers or competing jewellery retailers.

4. User Rights and Data Purge Requests

Under Section 14 of the Sri Lanka PDPA, you retain the full right to withdraw consent, inspect your stored information, or request an immediate and permanent purge of your email entry. To exercise this right, contact our Data Protection Officer at: aurumteam25@gmail.com.
